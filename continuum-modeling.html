Continuum Modeling

Discrete-to-continuum modeling of biological transport networks
← Back to Research

Summary

Biological transport networks—such as blood vessels, plant vasculature, or neuronal networks—are naturally modeled as metric graphs where edges carry flow or diffusive signals and nodes enforce conservation laws.

While this discrete picture captures topology, it becomes unwieldy for dense, spatially embedded systems (e.g., embryonic vasculature). We develop effective continuum descriptions that preserve the essential transport physics while enabling faster simulation and principled coarse-graining.
